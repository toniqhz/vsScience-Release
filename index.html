VsScience

A desktop app for working on your documents — Word, Excel, PDF, Outlook mail and Google Sheets, Docs, Slides — together with Claude, the AI assistant by Anthropic.

Download for Windows / macOS Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What VsScience does

VsScience is built for researchers, lecturers and office staff who are not programmers. You open a folder of documents on your computer, chat with Claude about them, and set up assistants ("agents") that handle routine paperwork such as preparing invitation emails, tracking a list of speakers in a spreadsheet or drafting a report.

Your documents, on your computer

Browse, preview and edit Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files in a familiar file-tree layout. Files stay on your machine.

Chat with Claude

Ask questions about your files, summarize, translate, compare versions, fill templates. Uses your own Claude account.

Agents for routine work

Assistants that follow your instructions on a schedule. Anything that leaves your computer (sending mail, editing shared files) waits for your approval unless you allow otherwise.

Outlook and Google

Optionally connect your Outlook mailbox and Google account so Claude can read mail, prepare drafts and work with your Sheets, Docs and Slides.

How VsScience uses your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. When you connect, VsScience asks for access to Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Slides so that you and your agents can:

  • Google Sheets — read a spreadsheet you share with the assistant (for example a list of conference speakers), add rows, update cells and create new spreadsheets.
  • Google Docs — read a document, append text, fill in template placeholders and create new documents.
  • Google Slides — read slide text and speaker notes, update text, add slides and create new presentations.

You sign in on Google's own page; your password never passes through VsScience. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer. Google data is used only to carry out what you ask in the app. VsScience has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data. Editing an existing file happens only with your permission.

VsScience's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. VsScience works with text in documents, mail and spreadsheets; it does not generate images and is not used to create any image content.

You can disconnect your account in the app at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
