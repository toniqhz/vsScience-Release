# Microsoft Store listing — English

## Description

VsScience lets you read books, documents and research papers and write exams together with Claude — right on the PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files on your computer.

Open a folder of documents, view a file on one side and chat with Claude on the other. Claude reads your documents, summarizes chapters, explains difficult passages, drafts review questions and tests, analyzes grade sheets, and edits and formats text. Every point Claude makes comes with a source link such as [document, p. 12] — click it to open the exact file at the exact page, with the quoted passage highlighted.

Made for teachers, students, researchers and anyone who works with documents a lot — no programming required.

What you can do:
• Summarize books, chapters and papers, with page numbers for each point
• Create multiple-choice and essay questions, and tests with answer keys
• Ask about an image or a passage: paste or drag images into the chat, or select part of Claude's answer to ask about it
• Analyze Excel spreadsheets
• Search the contents of every document in a folder
• Every change in the folder is tracked: compare with earlier versions and restore when needed
• Chats are saved inside the folder — copy the folder to another computer and continue where you left off
• Work from another computer: one machine keeps the documents, the other shares the same folder and chat
• Vietnamese interface with dark, light or automatic theme

Your documents stay on your computer. VsScience has no servers of its own and does not collect your data.

A Claude account (Pro or Max plan) is required to chat with Claude. Opening folders and viewing documents works without signing in.
